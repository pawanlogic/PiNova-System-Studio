QUICK NOTES

Good to know.

Does PiNova upload my system data?

No. The app stores history and settings locally on your computer. The website is a download page; it does not send your readings anywhere.

Does temperature monitoring work everywhere?

Not always. Raspberry Pi/Linux often expose a thermal reading. Many Windows PCs and Mac models do not provide a standard sensor interface, so the app may show N/A.

Why are the download links not ready yet?

The files must first be built on their target operating systems by GitHub Actions and published as a release. Follow the README included with the project.
